## Session Details

### S1: The Foundation (09:00–10:30)
- **Opening (15 min):** Welcome, logistics, framing — *"By end of day, explain Logos to anyone at PS tomorrow"*
- **What is Logos (75 min):**
  - Mission & Context (15 min) — what "revitalising civil society" means concretely
  - The Tech Stack (20 min) — Blockchain, Messaging, Storage and why these three
  - Specification-Driven Development (20 min) — what SDD is, why it matters, how everyone relates
  - Integration Map (15 min) — how the stack and specs connect
- **Owners:** Leadership + Tech leads; Corey owns SDD segment

### S2: The Path (11:00–12:30)
- **Presentation (60 min):** Testnet phases, what "launch" means, key dependencies, honest unknowns, preview of PS testnet reveal
- **Open Q&A (30 min):** Technical and strategic questions
- **Owners:** Technical leadership
- **Note:** Testnet demo reserved for PS Day 1 main stage. This gives internal context first.

### S3: The Expansion (14:00–15:30)
- **Who We're Building For (50 min):** Target communities, Circles strategic frame, "winnable issues," 2–3 steward stories (5–7 min each), EcoDev activation, Circles↔EcoDev connection
- **Internal Showcases (40 min):** Integration PoC, Circles use case implementation, spec visualization tool (if ready)
- **Owners:** EcoDev lead, 2–3 Circle stewards, demo owners TBD
- **Note:** No duplication with PS Day 1 Circles content (Culture Zone 11:00, Lab 10:00) or Jarrad's testnet demo (10:30)

### S4: The Focus (16:00–17:30)
- **Key Questions by Stream (50 min):**
  - Engineering: testnet participation target, EcoDev vs core roadmap prioritization, cross-team dependencies
  - Ecosystem Development: builder funnel, Circles→contributors pipeline, developer experience gaps
  - Community/Comms: core PS narrative, handling "when mainnet?", what stewards need from core
- **Synthesis + Bridge to PS (20 min):** Leadership reflects, PS venue orientation (9 zones, two buildings), logistics
- **The Charge (20 min):** Recap, memorable moment, energizing close

## Design Principles

- Single room, shared experience
- Pyramid structure (broad → specific)
- Pre-work assumed
- No PS duplication
- Coffee breaks = buffer time
