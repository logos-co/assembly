## Jacek's views
Jacek spent some time discussing and explaining specs and how they make his job easier at the Storage Offsite in Sevilla. I took some notes and wanted to elaborate on them with my perspective.

> Specs are the medium that research and engineering use to communicate. - Jacek

> A spec is the minimal document that allows me to derive everything I care about - Jacek

> A spec allows for a clean room implementation - Jacek

> A spec enables the ability to switch languages easily - Jacek

> A spec serves many purposes before software "is ready" - Jacek
