This section is dedicated to defining what Logos Improvement Proposals (LIPs) are and how they work within the Logos development process.

Specifications play a crucial role within FOSS development for a myriad of reasons, therefor it is crucial to have a solid understanding of what they are and how they relate to the work being done when building software in an asyncronous (and permissionless) environment. 

You can find various quotes and links that were given by IFT Leadership in [[LIPS Leadership Directives and notes]] 

A lot of content has been created around explaining what a "specification" is, and then how all technical work associated with it relates downstream to it in the [[meta-docs/lips/specs and tech docs/index|specs and tech docs]] section.
