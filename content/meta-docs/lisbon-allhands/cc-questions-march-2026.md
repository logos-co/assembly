## Process

> [!tip] Week 1 — Triage & route
> - Corey + Council sign off on category routing and nominated owners for each of the 9 buckets
> - Mark questions answered on stage by changing `open` → `stage` in the Status column
> - Each nominated owner acknowledges their category and agrees on a response format
> - Council decides which High-priority questions need a response within 2 weeks vs. 4 weeks

> [!tip] Weeks 2–4 — Answer production
> - **Engineering & architecture** — Logos Engineering FAQ doc (internal first, then public tech explainer for devs)
> - **Narrative & positioning** — Comms drafts, Council reviews; publish key answers to Logos Press Engine as a standalone piece
> - **Status app** — Status team dedicated recorded Q&A session (60 min); communities delivery question needs a direct, unfiltered answer
> - **Keycard** — Council formal written statement on budget/staffing; Keycard team handles tech questions separately
> - **Token & economics** — Council/Foundation statement; route through legal before any public phrasing
> - **Ecosystem dev & circles** — EcoDev async written responses; publish to Circles newsletter and internal wiki
> - **Coordination & process** — PM/Ops converts questions into tracked action items with owners; the answer *is* the action item
> - **Internal comms & tools** — IT/Ops quick wins list; resolve source-of-truth and info-stack questions first as they unblock everything else
> - **Org & people** — Council statement or policy memo; expertise/authority question warrants a principle-level response

> [!tip] Weeks 4–5 — Distribution
> - Single "All-Hands Follow-Up" document compiled from all answers, sent to all CCs
> - Public-facing subset published to Logos Press Engine (narrative, positioning, "why Logos" answers are good candidates)
> - All answers archived internally with links back to source sessions or docs
> - Status team Q&A and any other recorded sessions uploaded (YouTube unlisted or internal archive)

> [!tip] Week 6+ — Feedback & close
> - Short async survey to question submitters: "Was your question answered satisfactorily?"
> - Any unresolved questions rolled into the next quarterly town hall prep or All-Hands
> - Process audit: did every question get a route and a response format? Anything that fell through flagged to Council
