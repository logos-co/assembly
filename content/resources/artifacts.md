An artifact is anything tangible that gets created. It's that simple. 

Here's some examples of artifacts:
![[Pasted image 20251124123520.png]]

for any given artifact, we care about the following:
- who's working on it and the expectations of contribution for all involved
- estimated time of completion
- who the intended audience is
- the intended effect of its delivery to that audience
- once created, reasonable metrics to track the success of the intended effects. 

Sufficient information on all of those properties allows anybody to understand the complete lifecycle of a piece of content, and how to effectively plan for future content that's similar to it or for similar audiences.
