Transfer Info

Getting from Lisbon Airport (LIS) to the Hotels

Since this year's venue is quite close to the airport and Lisbon Airport is located within the city, there will be no organised transfers. See below for the easiest ways to get to Ikonik Lisboa and Eurostars Universal Lisboa.

Option 1

By Metro

  • Take the Red Line (Linha Vermelha) from Aeroporto Station (right at the airport, Arrivals Level)
  • Travel towards São Sebastião and get off at Cabo Ruivo Station
  • From Cabo Ruivo, it's about a 7-minute walk to Eurostars Universal Hotel
  • Tickets: Buy a single journey ticket or a reloadable Viva Viagem card at the machines in the station (cash or card). A single journey costs around €1.50–€2
Option 2

By Ride-Hailing (Uber or Bolt)

  • Both Uber and Bolt operate in Lisbon — pickup locations differ slightly:
    • Uber: Dedicated rideshare pickup on Level 2, P2 parking garage (Arrivals level). Follow signs for "Uber" or the "Kiss & Fly" zone after exiting arrivals
    • Bolt: Pickup at Parking Lot 2 (P2) on the Arrivals Level, just outside the main terminal in the outside parking area / short-stay lot
  • Uber is in the garage area, Bolt is in the outside parking area — both on the Arrivals level
  • Estimated journey time: ~15 minutes, depending on traffic
Option 3

By Car

  • The hotel offers 200 parking spots at €10 per day
  • Address for GPS: Eurostars Universal Hotel, Av. 5 de Outubro 107, 1050-059 Lisbon, Portugal
Tips: Taxis are also available at the airport, but Uber/Bolt is often cheaper and easier to track via the app. Lisbon traffic can be heavy during rush hours (8–10 AM and 5–7 PM), so consider extra time during these periods. The airport is relatively small and well-signposted — just follow "Metro" or "Ride-Hailing" signs.
